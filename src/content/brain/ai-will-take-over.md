---
title: A quick note on why AI is going to take over the world
date: 2026-10-24
description: Not a conspiracy theory, just an observation.
---

Quick one. I asked an AI to help me build this website and it did it in an afternoon. It picked the fonts, wrote the code and had opinions about my colour palette.

So yes, I think AI is going to take over the world. Not in a robots-with-lasers way. More in a "why would I ever do this myself again" way.

It starts with your website. Then your emails, then your calendar, and one day you look up and it's planning your trips and reminding you to meditate.

Honestly? Fine by me. It can have the emails. The squats stay mine.
