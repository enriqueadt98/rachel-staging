I wanted one place that felt like *mine*. Not a feed that disappears, not an algorithm deciding what people see. Just a quiet corner where I can keep track of what I'm reading, where I've been and what I'm working towards.

## What lives here

- **The brain:** random ideas, notes and things I want to remember.
- **Goals:** never more than three at a time.
- **Books and travels:** the good stuff.
